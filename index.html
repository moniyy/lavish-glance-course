Your questions.

Is this for beginners?

It's built for lash artists who already take clients and want to add the wet look properly. If you're completely new, the 1-to-1 in-person training is the better starting point.

Fully online and self-paced, so you can work through it around your clients. In-person 1-to-1 training is available separately.
